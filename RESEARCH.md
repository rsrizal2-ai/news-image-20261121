## 1. Kyiv & Poland — Russia's biggest raid of the war; missile lands in Poland

overnight, killing at least eight people in Kyiv and hitting bridges

on Polish territory; Prime Minister Tusk called it a deliberate
provocation while Kyiv's allies held a military exercise.

## 2. Wall Street — AI sell-off pushes US stocks into correction

## 3. Riyadh — three Houthi missiles shot down over the capital

missiles fired by Yemen's Houthis towards Riyadh early on Saturday

capital, two days after a Houthi strike damaged the PIF tower,
Riyadh's tallest skyscraper.

## 4. Barcelona — cancer pill trial: tumours undetectable in 1 in 12
Trial results presented at the EORTC-NCI-AACR symposium in Barcelona
show the daily pill daraxonrasib plus chemotherapy stopped tumours
growing in 90% of 61 patients with advanced pancreatic cancer, and
made tumours undetectable on scans in about 1 in 12 (8.2%).
Confirms the date: findings presented Friday 20 November at the
symposium and reported worldwide on 21 November 2026 (The Guardian
via thenews.pk, NY Post, Fox News, People — all 21 Nov 2026);
conference abstract presented 20 Nov 2026, press coverage 21 Nov.
